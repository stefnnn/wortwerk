# Translation platform pricing comparison

Snapshot: 10 October 2026. Prices are the vendors' advertised USD monthly rates unless noted; taxes, translation services, and unpriced add-ons are excluded. Annual-billing equivalents are used where vendors only publish an annual price. Prices and packaging change often, so treat this as a directional comparison and recheck vendor pages before making a buying decision.

## Normalization

Our comparison unit is **source keys**, with locales unlimited. Assumptions: 4 target locales by default and 5 words per translation. Thus 1,000 keys × 4 locales × 5 words = 20,000 translated words. Some competitors count each language copy (including the source language), while others count only source keys. That makes their “string” or “key” limits non-equivalent to our plan limits.

| Scenario      |                Source keys | Target locales | Users | Translation words (keys × locales × 5) |
| ------------- | -------------------------: | -------------: | ----: | -------------------------------------: |
| Small project |                      1,000 |              4 |     3 |                                 20,000 |
| Large project |                     10,000 |              6 |    10 |                                300,000 |
| Agency        | 100,000 across 10 projects |              4 |    20 |                              2,000,000 |

For meters that include the source language, use keys × (target locales + 1): 5,000, 70,000, and 500,000 strings respectively. For word meters that charge by hosted translations, the agency's 100,000 keys × 4 locales × 5 words gives 2,000,000 words. In practice, processed-word meters can depend on annual changes or translation activity, not just the size of the catalog; those rows are capacity-fit estimates, not guaranteed bills.

### Large project and agency price snapshot

Among vendors with a numeric USD estimate in the table, the **large project** spans about **$96–$1,049/month**, with a median of **$260/month** (Crowdin, Localazy, POEditor, Phrase, Lokalise). This excludes Hosted Weblate at **€114/month** and vendors with quote-only pricing. For the **agency**, only Crowdin (**$667/month**) and Localazy (**$438/month annual-billing equivalent**) have numeric USD estimates, giving a provisional **$438–$667/month** span and **$553/month** median; this is a two-vendor median, so it is not representative of the quote-based enterprise market. Hosted Weblate is **€193/month**. Phrase, Lokalise, POEditor above its 100k tier, Transifex, and Smartling require a quote or custom pricing at this scale.

## Plans and practical limits

- **Lokalise:** Explorer is $149/mo annual, up to 5 advanced seats (10 max with add-ons), 5 projects, 10 total languages, and 60k processed words/year. Growth is $379/mo, 10 advanced seats, unlimited projects/languages, and 225k processed words/year. Advanced starts at $1,049/mo, 15 advanced seats and 1m processed words/year. Enterprise is quote-based, 40 seats and 3m processed words/year. Plan quotas are hard limits unless topped up. Its new published model distinguishes unlimited hosted words from processed words.
- **Crowdin:** Individual/team plans and Crowdin Enterprise are separate products. Hosted words are aggregate across projects and equal source words multiplied by target languages; extra capacity is available. Paid plans allow unlimited translators and proofreaders, while project, manager, hosted-word, and enterprise workflow allowances vary. Eligible open-source/academic projects can apply for free licenses.
- **Phrase:** Software UI/UX is $525/mo annual, 15 Strings seats, 1m Strings managed words, unlimited languages, and 5 Strings projects. Team is $1,245/mo annual, 20 Strings seats, unlimited TMS seats, unlimited projects, and 1.2m Strings managed words; Business is custom and raises capacity to 3m words/150 Strings seats. Strings managed words and TMS processed words are separate meters.
- **POEditor:** Free includes 1,000 strings and up to 5 contributors; Start $20/mo/3,000; Plus $60/10,000; Premium $160/30,000; Enterprise $260/100,000, then custom. “Strings” means the sum of terms and translations, so adding languages increases usage. Paid plans include unlimited contributors; feature gates include workflows, SSO, audit logs, and automation.
- **Transifex:** The current AI-First offer removes subscription tiers and advertises unlimited stored content, projects, and collaborators. The charge is for source words actually translated by its AI; human review is excluded. It does not include third-party machine translation. Price is sales-quoted, so catalog size alone cannot produce a quote.
- **Localazy:** Professional starts at 1,000 source keys ($34/mo annual, $41 monthly), Autopilot scales through 7,500 keys, and Business from 10,000 to 100,000 keys ($175–$438/mo annual equivalents). All tiers advertise unlimited projects, users, languages, and words; paid translation/AI services draw from monthly credits, with extra credits sold separately. The source-key limit aggregates projects.
- **Hosted Weblate:** Public cloud tiers are 10k (€47/mo), 40k (€70), 160k (€114), 640k (€193), and 2.56m (€342) hosted strings. Hosted strings count all languages including source; a “string” is a translation-format text unit and may be a word, phrase, or paragraph. All features, projects, and translators are included. Libre open-source projects may qualify for a free plan up to 160k strings.
- **Smartling:** Core is free to start for basic document workflows. Enterprise adds customizable workflows, broad integrations, advanced project/user management, unlimited translation memory, linguistic assets, reporting and LQA. The platform price is quote-based. Public per-word translation rates apply to services, not the software subscription.

## Summary

For the large project, the listed USD estimates span **$96–$1,049/month**, with a median of **$260/month** across five vendors. For the agency, only Crowdin and Localazy have numeric USD estimates: **$438–$667/month**, with a two-vendor median of **$553/month**. Hosted Weblate is listed separately at **€193/month**. Quote-only vendors are excluded, so the agency median is especially tentative.
